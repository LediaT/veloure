About us

Crafted by hand. Inspired by nature.

Velouré is a celebration of everything dessert should be: silky, rich, and made with intention. In our Bergen County kitchen, each creation is shaped by hand in small batches, with natural ingredients chosen for their purity and flavor.

Vegan, gluten-free, and free of refined sugar, our desserts are made to be shared by everyone in the family, at every table and for every occasion.
